# Statement correspondence

## Literal JSP-001017

The published catalog asks for an infinite path in the lattice graph of coprime coordinate pairs that avoids vertices whose two coordinates are both prime.

`JSP001017.jsp_001017_literal` proves exactly that literal reading.

## Stronger source problem

Erdős #1212 additionally excludes vertices with a coordinate equal to `1`: both coordinates are required to exceed `1`, with at least one composite coordinate at every vertex. This repository makes no claim about that stronger problem.
